What is the model actually doing?

It receives a structured board, the next pieces, and every legal vertical-drop placement. The rules calculate each placement’s resulting lines, holes and height; the model chooses the placement. No screenshots, path planner or best-move fallback.

A poor choice stays in the game. A refusal or error pauses it. Reset, pause and manual changes invalidate an in-flight result.
